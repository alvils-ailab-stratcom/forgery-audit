# Resemble forgery report

Delivered format, fixed by the user: `results/<case>/reports/<file>.lv.md` per artifact with three sections
generated by `make reports`: **Materiāls** (file, SHA-256, format, date, analysis id), **Veiktās pārbaudes
un rezultāti** (one row per check with Resemble's own labels, scores, certainty, watermark and C2PA outcome,
source tracing, reverse search sources, Intelligence assessments with confidences, abnormalities,
transcription, question count, EXIF or container metadata) and **Secinājums**, which is your
`conclusion.lv.txt`, followed by **Atsauces** (numbered references generated from the evidence: reverse-image-search
articles with resolved URL, similarity and provider reason; the Resemble analysis id; documentation of each
method) and **Rādītāju skaidrojums** (score legend). No images, no limitations section. References are
mandatory. The cover `atzinums.lv.md` repeats the format, verdict and key rows per file.

Write in English while analyzing; deliver Latvian. Read `analysis.en.json`, `latest.json`,
`questions.en.json`, `metadata.json`, look at the media and `visualizations/` before writing.

Conclusion rules: three short paragraphs, roughly 250–320 words, clear and concise, no filler. State what
was tried and what came back, quoting key numbers with a decimal comma ("rezultāts 0,995", "digitāla
pārveidošana 75"). Attribute provider statements ("Resemble AI detektors klasificē", "Intelligence
norāda"); when two runs disagree, say so and do not pick a side without your own observation. Keep visual
and audio findings separate. Sampled frames are not a continuous interval. Audio source tracing names a
service, not a model or account. Absent watermarks or C2PA never prove authenticity; say what they do
mean. Filesystem dates are not capture dates; EXIF and container tags are editable. Platform watermarks,
handles and "AI-generated" captions visible in the frames are reportable observations.

Cite sources by name in the prose ("divas Jauns.lv publikācijas, saites atsaucēs"), never by pasting URLs.
Cover the seven MIC questions in order inside the flow (deepfake used; which part; manipulated versus
generated; technology; task or prompt; source materials; when, where, device). Combine unanswerable
questions into one sentence; never invent answers. Finish with one sentence on whether the independent
checks agree.

Verify after regeneration: the report renders and the prose matches the table.
