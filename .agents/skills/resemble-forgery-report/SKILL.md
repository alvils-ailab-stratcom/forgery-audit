# Resemble forgery report

Delivered format, fixed by the user: `results/<case>/reports/<file>.lv.md` and `.lv.pdf` per artifact, an
official document generated by `make reports`: title and header table (document number, date, issuer,
file, SHA-256, format, analysis id); **1. Atzinums**, which is your `conclusion.lv.txt`; **2. Veiktās
pārbaudes un rezultāti** (one row per check with Resemble's own labels, scores, certainty, watermark and
C2PA outcome, source tracing, reverse search sources, Intelligence assessments with confidences,
abnormalities, transcription, question count, EXIF or container metadata); **3. Atsauces**
(reverse-image-search articles with resolved URL, similarity and provider reason; the Resemble analysis
id; documentation of each method); **4. Rādītāju skaidrojums** (one bullet per score type); signature
block. No file-name subheading, no images, no limitations section, and the client's examination
questions are internal guidance that is never printed. References are mandatory. The cover
`atzinums.lv.md`/`.pdf` is generated only.

Write in English while analyzing; deliver Latvian. Read `analysis.en.json`, `latest.json`,
`questions.en.json`, `metadata.json`, look at the media and `visualizations/` before writing.

Conclusion rules: three short paragraphs, roughly 250–320 words, clear and concise, no filler. State what
was tried and what came back, quoting key numbers with a decimal comma ("rezultāts 0,995", "digitāla
pārveidošana 75"). Attribute provider statements ("Resemble AI detektors klasificē", "Intelligence
norāda"); when two runs disagree, say so and do not pick a side without your own observation. Keep visual
and audio findings separate. Sampled frames are not a continuous interval. Audio source tracing names a
service, not a model or account. Absent watermarks or C2PA never prove authenticity; say what they do
mean. Filesystem dates are not capture dates; EXIF and container tags are editable. Platform watermarks,
handles and "AI-generated" captions visible in the frames are reportable observations.

Cite sources by name in the prose ("divas Jauns.lv publikācijas, saites atsaucēs"), never by pasting URLs.
Answer the seven internal examination questions in order inside the flow: whether deepfake technology was
used; which part exactly; manipulated versus generated; which technology; what task the technology was
given; which source materials; when, where and with which device. Combine unanswerable
questions into one sentence; never invent answers. Finish with one sentence on whether the independent
checks agree.

Verify after regeneration: the report renders and the prose matches the table.
